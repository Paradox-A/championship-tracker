EFL Championship 2026-27 Tracker

Matchday 8 · Last updated September 19, 2026 21:16 UTC
New to the Championship? This is the second tier of English football, one level below the Premier League. 24 clubs play 46 games each. The top 2 go straight up to the Premier League; teams finishing 3rd-6th enter the play-offs for a third promotion spot; the bottom 3 are relegated down to League One. It's widely considered one of the most competitive and financially high-stakes leagues in the world, since promotion to the Premier League is worth well over £100m to a club.

Promotion Race

Finishing 1st or 2nd means automatic promotion to the Premier League — no extra games needed. Finishing 3rd-6th means the play-offs: two single-leg semi-finals (3rd vs 6th, 4th vs 5th), then a final at Wembley Stadium for the last promotion spot. The play-off final is sometimes called the "richest game in football" because of what promotion is worth.

Defensive Strength: Clean Sheets

A clean sheet is a game where a team doesn't concede at all. It's the single clearest sign of defensive solidity — Championship promotion winners are almost always among the league's clean-sheet leaders, because a stingy defense is the most reliable way to grind out points over a brutal 46-game season.

Home Fortress vs. Road Warriors

Some teams are much stronger at home than away (or the reverse) — this is one of the oldest storylines in football, and it matters more in the Championship than almost any other league because of the sheer number of games. Points per game (PPG) at home vs. away shows exactly how lopsided that split is. Form is the last 5 results (most recent last) — a better read on momentum than the season-long record.
